Why the Answers Are Average

Garbage In, Garbage Out

A vague instruction gets a vague answer. If you cannot state precisely what you need, the model will give you something that sounds right and says nothing.

Talking to it like a search box

Keywords worked on Google. A model needs structure — a role, a task, the context, the shape of the output.

No role assigned

Without a persona the model answers as nobody in particular. Give it a role and it can take a position, and change it when you ask.

Our Methodology

The Craft of Asking

The PTCF prompt structure

Persona, Task, Context, Format. Four slots that turn "write me something about X" into an instruction with only one reasonable reading.

Chain-of-thought reasoning

Make the model show its working. For decisions with several moving parts, the reasoning is the part you need to check — not the conclusion.

Knowledge bases and RAG

Turn a thousand pages of internal documents into something you can ask questions of, with answers that cite the page they came from.
