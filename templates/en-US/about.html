Who Builds This

Empowering Business with AI

TauX builds GEO — Generative Engine Optimization — and the AI systems around it. We work from Kaohsiung, close to the manufacturers and operators we build for, and we ship to a standard that holds up anywhere.

The name comes from the Greek letter τ — Tau, the time constant in control theory and physics: how long a system takes to respond to a change and settle into its new state. What TauX works on is a company's time constant for AI. Not whether it adapts, but how long it takes.

Our Mission

Generative AI is changing where traffic comes from. Our job is to move brands from ranking in a list to being the answer — structuring content so an AI reads it, understands it, and cites it.

What We Build With

High-performance backends in Go, modern front-ends, and the plumbing between them. Data governance through to autonomous agents — we build the parts that have to keep working after the demo.
