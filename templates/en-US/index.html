TauX turns AI into systems a business can run on, rather than a demo that impresses once.

The name comes from the Greek letter τ — Tau, the time constant in physics and control engineering: how quickly a system responds to a change and settles again. TauX works on a company's time constant for AI — not whether it adapts, but how long that takes, and how steady it is on the way through.
