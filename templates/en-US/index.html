02 — Questions

Common Questions

How is GEO different from SEO?
SEO is about being found — a place among the blue links near the top. GEO is about being the answer the AI gives, and the source it cites for it. One buys you a ticket to the room. The other puts you on stage.

What does it cost?
It depends on scope. A brochure site usually lands between NT$150,000 and NT$500,000; a custom management system between NT$300,000 and NT$1,500,000. The consultation is free — we would rather understand the work before putting a number on it.

How long does it take?
It depends on size. A website is typically two to four weeks, a short video one to two. Once requirements are settled you get a dated schedule, not an estimate.

What happens after it ships?
No. Every software project includes a year of technical support, and there is a maintenance agreement after that if you want one. We are more use to you as a long-term technical partner than as a one-off supplier.

How do we start?
Email hello@taux.io and tell us what you are trying to do. Then it is: a conversation about requirements → a proposal with a number attached → a start date.
