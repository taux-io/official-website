What We Do

GEO is not SEO under a new name. It asks a different question: when a language model goes to answer someone, can your content be retrieved, understood, and quoted without the model having to guess?

Structured data

Schema markup, entity identification, knowledge-graph relationships. So a machine does not have to infer who you are, what you sell and who wrote it from the layout.

Chunking and quotability

Content restructured into pieces a model can lift: consistent shape, explicit definitions, each passage able to stand on its own when quoted out of context.

Crawler access and llms.txt

An audit of what AI crawlers can reach, and an llms.txt that states which content is open to models and on what terms.

Measuring citations

A baseline and then tracking against it: how often the major engines mention and cite you, and whether that is moving.

How the Work Runs

1

Audit

Structured data, content structure, crawler access and entity declarations — plus a quotability baseline recorded before anything is touched. Without a baseline there is no way to tell afterwards whether the work did anything.

2

Implementation

Entity declarations and structured data corrected, content re-chunked, llms.txt written. Shipped as one change, so the effect can be attributed to it.

3

Measurement

After six to eight weeks, measured against the baseline — the actual change, not a claim about it. With automated checks so what was fixed does not quietly rot at the next redesign.
